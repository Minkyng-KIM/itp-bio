What CDThera does

Clinically validated AI model
AI risk-prediction model reaches internal AUC 0.882, with external validation on two independent cohorts (AUC 0.847 and 0.835).
Non-invasive, repeatable screening
An at-home oral-saliva collection kit analyzing dementia-related biomarkers (Aβ42/40, p-Tau; Advanced adds BDNF and Calprotectin), processed into a personalized 0–100 dementia risk score.
Platform extensibility
A single antibody-chip + AI + longitudinal-monitoring platform architected to scale from dementia into cardiovascular disease and other chronic conditions.
