Products & technology

MSoC (Metabolic System on Chip)
Interconnects multiple organs via microfluidic channels on a single chip, enabling ADME profiling and high-precision PK/PD evaluation without animal models — scalable across 2-, 3-, 4-, and multi-organ configurations.
SoC Flow™ / Gravity Machine
A precision tilting system that generates controlled, bidirectional fluid flow in MSoC platforms without external pumps — enabling stable, reproducible multiorgan culture through automated angle and flow control.
3-in-1 emulate™ platform
An all-in-one platform with built-in AI-based organoid differentiation, culture, and drug-efficacy evaluation automation software.
