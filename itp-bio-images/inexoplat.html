Why it matters

Human IEP-01 targets high-unmet-need solid tumors, with pancreatic cancer — roughly 510K annual global cases — as the lead indication. Most current immunotherapies rely on downstream T-cell checkpoint blockade alone, which fails in "immune-cold" tumors.

What INEXOPLAT does

Dual-value translational platform
IEP-01V is developed in naturally occurring canine/feline cancers as an independent veterinary oncology product, while generating clinically relevant evidence to support human IEP-01 translation.
TME-reprogramming mechanism
IEP-01 activates innate immunity through DC/macrophage modulation, reprogramming an immunosuppressive "cold" tumor microenvironment toward an immune-active state.
Shared manufacturing platform
IEP-01V and IEP-01 share a common innate-immune mechanism and a scalable EV manufacturing platform with proprietary producer cells and integrated CMC capabilities.
Dual-track development
IEP-01V (Veterinary)Phase 1 planned, 2027
IEP-01 (Human)IND filing planned, 2028–29
IEP-01V provides near-term clinical validation ahead of human IEP-01.
